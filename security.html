Malware and Cyber Threats

The Morris Worm

The Morris Worm was realeased on November 2,1988 and quickly spread across early Internet. Within 24 hours, it had infected about 6,000 of the roughly 60,000 computers connected to the Internet at the time. The worm slowed important university and government computer systems, delayed emails, and caused damage that was estimated from $100,000 into the millions. The attack become an important part in cybersecurity history because it showed how vulnerable computer networks could be and helped create one of the first computer emergency response teams.
