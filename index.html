Management Information Systems (MIS) (MIS) and Artificial Intelligence (AI) have changed the way businesses and organizations operate, manage, and use data. Managers use MIS reports, such as sales summaries, inventory levels, and budget tracking, to make better decisions for the company. MIS is most valuable when it is used by people who understand how to manage and apply the information effectively. AI has made MIS even more useful by analyzing patterns, identifying changes in customer behavior, and recommending possible actions. AI can also help reduce human error and improve efficiency by completing certain tasks more quickly. As technology continues to evolve, AI and MIS are becoming more connected and are helping businesses improve efficiency in many different areas.
