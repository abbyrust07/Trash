Correct AI Usage

The correct usage for AI is for assistance in understanding concepts, making practice problems and using it to proofread your work. This still allows you to continue to use and grow your critical thinking skills while also learning new things. AI is also a great way to create new practice problems to continue to understand concepts or use to study before an exam.

Your reasoning is strong because you explain that AI should support learning rather than replace the student's own thinking. You also give specific examples, such as using AI to explain concepts, create practice problems, and proofread work. One improvement would be to mention that students should still check AI responses for accuracy because AI can make mistakes or give incomplete information. Another perspective is that even helpful uses of AI can become a problem if a student relies on it too much, so it is important to use AI as a study tool while still completing the main thinking and learning independently.

Benefits of AI

  1. Helps save time by completing tasks more efficiently and without human error
  2. Helps people understand and practice concepts for learning purposes

Who Benefits?

  1. Students who use AI for learning and studying
  2. Businesses and Organizations that use AI to improve efficiency and decision making

How AI Tools Used

  • Generating practice questions, study material, and brainstorming ideas
  • Proofreading writing and giving feedback

Incorrect AI Usage

Cheating
Using AI to complete an assignment without doing the work yourself.

Copying
Submitting AI-generated information as your own work without permission.

Ignoring Class Rules
Continuing the use of AI even after stated in syllabus that AI is not allowed.

AI can be helpful but it can also be used incorrectly. Using AI to complete your work can inhibit your ability of learning concepts in school. AI can also inhibit your ability to think critically and problem solve outside of school. Students should responsibly use AI while also following class rules.
